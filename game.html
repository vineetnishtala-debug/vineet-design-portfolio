WEEK 03 / PROCESS NOTE

From restoring
to constructing.

FEEDBACK I FOCUSED ON

The first draft’s concept was clearer in its text than in its imagery. The main feedback asked for recognizable photographs, specific people and objects, fragments with different narrative implications, visible caption changes, and an explicit moment when the system reuses the player’s edits as evidence.

WHAT I CHANGED

I rebuilt all three archive scenes as detailed family-photo collages, made every fragment alter both the image and caption, added a clear record-update screen after each choice, saved the player’s selections, and reused those exact selections in File 03. The confidence score can now rise even when evidence becomes less reliable.

AI + MY DECISIONS

I used AI to help organize the JavaScript state, test alternate captions, and identify unclear transitions. I chose the family-archive story, designed the visual system, wrote the final narrative details, decided how each fragment changes the record, and kept uncertainty—not a correct answer—as the central experience.
